FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers regarding contracts, code ownership, security, and ongoing SLA support.

Who owns the intellectual property and source code? expand_more
You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, data pipelines, model weights, and custom design assets upon final milestone completion. VerHost provides full git repository transfers and deployment documentation.
How do you protect proprietary data during AI model fine-tuning? expand_more
We operate under strict zero-data-egress protocols. When training predictive models or building internal RAG chatbots, models run in dedicated, isolated virtual private clouds (VPCs). Your corporate documents and customer records are never used to train public models.
What is your typical project kickoff turnaround? expand_more
Once initial scoping is approved, our technical discovery sprint commences within 3 to 5 business days. Fast-track expedited engagements can mobilize within 48 hours for mission-critical timelines.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance and SLA support post-launch? expand_more
Yes. Every project includes 30 days of warranty and hypercare monitoring. We also offer dedicated ongoing SLA support retainers covering continuous cluster maintenance, 99.98% uptime monitoring, model retraining, and feature expansions.
Can VerHost collaborate alongside our internal engineering team? expand_more
Absolutely. We frequently operate as an embedded specialized unit—handling specific high-complexity initiatives such as a predictive ML pipeline, low-latency WebSocket infrastructure, or a headless redesign while integrating with your existing sprint rituals.
